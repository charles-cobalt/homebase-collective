OUR ORIGIN

Home Base began in the in-between: the season where one chapter ends before the next has taken shape. Again and again we saw the same gap - inner work unsupported by the spaces people return to, and beautiful rooms disconnected from the lives inside them. We believed there was another way: consider the person and the place together.
